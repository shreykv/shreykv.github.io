About

I’m a product-focused engineer who likes tight feedback loops: prototype → measure → learn → ship. My work sits at the intersection of sensors, validation, and data—building systems that collect signals reliably, turn them into insights, and drive better decisions in the real world.

I care about practical outcomes: test plans, clear metrics, traceable experiments, and robust implementations. If it can’t be measured, it can’t be improved.
